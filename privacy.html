Privacy Policy

Zero Log, Complete Anonymity

🔒

True Zero-Log Commitment

We do not store, write, or log any messages, call logs, metadata, or media content. Our servers operate entirely in-memory.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Because XOXO is built on a Zero-Storage Architecture, we do not require, request, or store:

  • Your real name, email address, phone number, or social profiles.
  • IP addresses, browser footprints, or device registration data.
  • Chat history, shared photos, or audio recordings (all base64 image data and WebRTC signal payloads are forwarded in real-time and cleared immediately from server memory).

2. How WebRTC Video Calls Work

Our servers only act as a WebRTC signaling facilitator. Once a video connection is established, the stream is directly Peer-to-Peer (P2P) between your browser and the stranger's browser. Video and audio packets never pass through or get recorded by our servers.

3. Client-Side Encryption

Private chats and group rooms are secured using client-side encryption. The keys are stored locally in your browser session and are never transmitted to our servers in plaintext, protecting your conversations from interception.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external web portals.
